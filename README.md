# Hike Footwear: CRO Case Study

**Full-funnel conversion rate optimization audit for a DTC barefoot shoe brand**  
Conducted via Osmosia · Emma Larroze

---

## Overview

Hike Footwear is a Shopify-based DTC brand selling health-focused barefoot shoes to 1.45M+ customers, driven primarily by Meta Ads traffic. Despite strong brand credibility and genuine social proof, conversion rates were underperforming relative to traffic volume and media spend.

This case study documents the full CRO audit: funnel diagnostic, persona mapping, 5 strategic levers, quick wins, A/B test roadmap, and projected business impact.

**The finding:** The problem was not acquisition. It was conversion architecture.

## Key Findings

- **Funnel leak:** Drop-off concentrated at PDP → Add-to-Cart (high friction)
- **Root cause:** No pain-to-solution narrative, hidden size guide, trust signals absent at checkout, mobile experience not prioritized
- **5 levers:** PDP restructuring, inline size guide, bundle reframe, checkout trust continuity, mobile-first redesign
- **6 quick wins:** No A/B test required, implementable immediately
- **Projected impact:** +$84K/month (conservative) to +$188K/month (optimistic), with zero additional ad spend

---

## Methodology

Full-funnel audit based on:
- Heatmap & session recording analysis
- Funnel drop-off mapping
- Persona-led copy & UX review
- Competitive benchmarking (DTC footwear & health verticals)
- A/B test prioritization by impact × feasibility
